Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Zero Ad-Tracker Commitment

At Nexus Gaming, we prioritize player privacy above all else. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not integrate invasive third-party ad networks or behavioral profiling scripts.

2. Identity Authentication via Auth0

When you sign up or log in, authentication is powered by Auth0 (Okta). We only store your public profile name, email, and security identifier to keep your cloud progress and game scores synchronized. Sensitive credentials and passwords are encrypted and processed exclusively within Auth0's secure infrastructure.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser LocalStorage and essential session cookies strictly for gameplay state (high scores, audio preferences, active session tokens, and cookie consent preferences). You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Security & Rate Limiting

To defend the arena against denial-of-service and bot attacks, our systems monitor request velocity and enforce token-bucket rate limits (HTTP 429). Anomaly logs contain no personally identifiable data and are retained purely for diagnostic stability.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your operative handle and associated game history, transmit a request via our Transmission Center.
